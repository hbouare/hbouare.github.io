**The problem.** Running online prize draws demands verifiable fairness and transparency — without it, participant trust collapses — while staying simple to operate.

**The approach.** A platform where users create accounts, buy tickets and follow their participation history, backed by a secure random-generation engine that guarantees draw integrity. A back-office configures campaigns, publishes results and tracks transactions.
